# Manual Surface Book 3 Checklist
Check for swelling/display lifting, dead pixels, touch/ghost touch, keyboard, trackpad, cameras, Windows Hello, speakers, microphones, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, USB-A, USB-C, SD, audio, Surface Connect, detach/reattach, GPU return, charger stability, serial/ownership and organization lock.

Walk away for swelling, display lifting, missing GPU, detach failure, intermittent charging, SSD failure, repeated hardware errors or unexplained shutdowns.
